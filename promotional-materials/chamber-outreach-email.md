## Phone Script for Chamber Outreach

### Opening

"Hi [Name], this is Nigel Lear from STL Business Guide. I'm a local business owner here in St. Louis, and I built a business directory focused exclusively on our metro area. I'm calling because I think there might be a great partnership opportunity with [Chamber Name] that could really benefit your members. Do you have a quick two minutes, or should I call back at a better time?"

### If They Have Time

"Great, thank you. Here's the short version: I built STL Business Guide because I was tired of seeing local businesses get lost on those giant national directories. You know the ones - you're on page 47 competing with companies from California and New York.

Our directory is St. Louis-only. Every listing is reviewed for quality. And most importantly, we never sell contact information to spammers.

Your members can list for free, or upgrade to Premium or VIP for more visibility. And I wanted to explore partnership opportunities - things like special member benefits, co-marketing, or even a custom landing page for your chamber members.

Would you be open to a brief conversation about what that might look like?"

### Handling Objections

**"We already have a member directory"**
"That's great - you should definitely keep that. This isn't a replacement, it's an additional channel. Think of it like having both a website and a Facebook page. Different people find you in different places. Plus, being in a public directory means non-members can discover your members too."

**"Our members don't need another directory"**
"I hear that. But here's what I'm hearing from business owners: they're paying for listings on national sites and getting nothing but spam calls. They want something local that actually works. This gives them another option without replacing anything they're already doing."

**"We're not looking for new partnerships right now"**
"I understand. Would it be okay if I sent you some information to review when you are looking? And maybe check back in a few months?"

### Closing

"Would you have 15 minutes next week for a more detailed conversation? I can show you the platform and we can explore what a partnership might look like. I'm flexible on timing - whatever works for your schedule."

**What Members Get:**
- Free Premium upgrade for first month

**Cost:** Custom agreement
