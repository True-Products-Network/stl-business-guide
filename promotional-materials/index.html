Email Sequence

5-email sequence for your 10,000 contact list

Email 1: Launch Announcement

Day 1

Subject: St. Louis Business Guide is Live - Get Your Business Listed (Free)

Introduces the directory, explains the three tiers (Free/Premium/VIP), and includes CTA to submit listing.

Email 2: Follow-Up

Day 3-4

Subject: Did you see this? Free business listing for St. Louis

Short follow-up for non-openers. Brief value prop and link.

Email 3: Value-Focused

Day 7

Subject: The problem with most business directories (and what I did about it)

Problem/solution framework. Contrasts big national directories vs STL Business Guide.

Email 4: Social Proof

Day 14

Subject: [First Name], businesses are already getting found

Shares stats and testimonials. Creates urgency about early listing advantages.

Email 5: Final Call

Day 21

Subject: Last call: Free listings won't stay this open forever

Final opportunity. Soft urgency. Option to opt-out of future emails.

Tip: Remove subscribers who submit a listing from the sequence. Set up automation to pause sequence on conversion.
