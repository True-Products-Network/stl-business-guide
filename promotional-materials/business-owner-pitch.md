# Business Owner Pitch Guide
## How to Explain STL Business Guide to Potential Listers

---

## The 30-Second Elevator Pitch

"STL Business Guide is a local business directory built specifically for St. Louis area businesses. Unlike those giant national sites where you get buried on page 47, we're focused entirely on helping local customers find local businesses. We offer free listings, and premium options if you want more visibility and features like analytics and priority placement. It takes 5 minutes to set up, and your listing goes live after a quick quality review."

---

## The 2-Minute Explanation

**The Problem:**
"Most business directories are national platforms where you're competing with companies from all over the country. You're on page 47, surrounded by outdated listings, and the only calls you get are from salespeople who bought your contact info."

**The Solution:**
"STL Business Guide is different. We're local-only - just St. Louis metro area businesses. Every listing is reviewed by a real person. You control your information and can update it anytime. And we never sell your contact info to third parties."

**How It Works:**
"You submit your business info - takes about 5 minutes. I review it for quality and accuracy, usually same day. Then your listing goes live and local customers can find you when they search."

**The Options:**
"Free listings are actually free - no credit card, no trial period, no games. If you want more visibility, Premium is $47 a month and gets you priority placement, social media links, and business hours. VIP is $97 a month and adds analytics so you can see exactly how many people are viewing your listing and clicking to contact you."

**The Ask:**
"Want to get your business listed? I can send you the link and you'll be live within 24 hours."

## Handling Common Objections

### "I already have a Google Business Profile"

**Response:** "Great! You should definitely keep that. STL Business Guide isn't a replacement - it's an additional place to be found. Think of it like this: some people search Google, some people prefer browsing a curated local directory. Being in both places means more chances to be found. Plus, you get a backlink to your website which helps with SEO."

**Key Point:** Complementary, not competitive

---

### "I don't have time to manage another listing"

**Response:** "I get it - you're busy running your business. That's why we made this simple. The initial setup takes 5 minutes, and then you're done unless you want to update something. Compare that to social media where you need to post constantly. This is set-it-and-forget-it marketing."

**Key Point:** Low time investment, ongoing benefit

---

### "I'm not technical"

**Response:** "You don't need to be. The submission form is simple - just fill in your business name, description, contact info, and pick a category. If you can fill out a form, you can do this. And if you get stuck, I'm here to help."

**Key Point:** No technical skills required

---

### "I tried directories before and got nothing but spam"

**Response:** "That's exactly why I built this differently. Most directories make money by selling your contact information. We don't do that. Your info stays private. The only people who see it are potential customers browsing the directory. And every listing is reviewed to keep quality high and spam low."

**Key Point:** Privacy protection, quality control

---

### "Is the free listing really free?"

**Response:** "Yes, really. No credit card required. No 30-day trial that auto-converts. No hidden fees. It's just free. I offer Premium and VIP for businesses that want more features, but the free listing is a complete, working listing that helps you get found."

**Key Point:** No strings attached

---

### "How do I know it works?"

**Response:** "Fair question. With VIP listings, you get an analytics dashboard that shows exactly how many people view your listing and click to contact you. You'll know it's working because you'll see the numbers. And since it's free to start, you can try it and see what happens before spending anything."

**Key Point:** Measurable results, low risk

---

### "I'm already paying for advertising elsewhere"

**Response:** "Smart - you should diversify your marketing. This isn't about replacing what works, it's about adding another channel. For $0 (free) or $47-97/month, you're getting ongoing visibility. Compare that to a single Facebook ad campaign or a one-time print ad. This is ongoing presence for minimal investment."

**Key Point:** Additional channel, cost-effective

## Key Phrases That Work

### Lead with the benefit:
- "Get found by local customers"
- "5 minutes to set up, works 24/7"
- "Free visibility in a St. Louis-focused directory"

### Address the fear:
- "No spam, no games, no selling your info"
- "You control your listing"
- "Cancel anytime, no contracts"

### Create urgency (softly):
- "Search results favor listings that are already getting engagement"
- "Early listings get more visibility as the directory grows"
- "Takes 5 minutes - why not try it?"

### Social proof:
- "Other [their industry] businesses are already listed"
- "We're adding [X] new businesses per week"
- "Businesses are already getting calls from the directory"

## Follow-Up Strategy

### If They Say "I Need to Think About It"

**Response:** "Of course. I'll send you the link so you have it when you're ready. Can I check back with you next week?"

**Then:**
1. Send the link immediately
2. Follow up in 3-4 days with a quick text/email
3. If no response, follow up once more after a week
4. Add them to your general nurture sequence

---

### If They Say "Not Right Now"

**Response:** "No problem. Can I check back in a month or two? The directory is growing quickly, and I don't want you to miss out."

**Then:**
1. Set a reminder to follow up
2. Keep them on your radar for events, networking, etc.
3. When you follow up, mention new features or businesses that joined
